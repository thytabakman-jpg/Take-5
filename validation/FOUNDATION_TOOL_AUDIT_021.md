## Takeover independence

The successor must survive loss of the predecessor repository.

Required closure:
RuntimeLaw(Take5) is internally reconstructible from Take5 artifacts;
no runtime import/fetch of Reaserch is required;
historical evidence needed for future audit is copied as provenance-bearing snapshot rather than dynamically depended upon;
updates arrive as candidate deltas and pass Currentness Audit + admission, not as live dependency on the predecessor.

Implemented foundation snapshot:
runtime/foundation_snapshot.py.

The predecessor remains useful as historical provenance until migration, but it is not allowed to be a runtime dependency.

## Fixed point

No whole-system rebuild is supported.
Material foundation changes identified by the per-tool MT/Architecture/RTC sweep are the configured-run wrapper, mode-cube upgrade, missing centers, emergent admission, and takeover snapshot.

Next closure requires regression after these patches plus recursive currentness reentry.
