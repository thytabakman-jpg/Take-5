# Take-5 Promotion Benchmark

Question: has Take-5 earned the right to replace Reaserch?

## Historical benchmarks
B1 wrong-object recovery
B2 constraint persistence
B3 hidden semantic primitive
B4 cross-project isolation
B5 semantic/runtime separation
B6 broad-corpus navigation

## Successor-specific requirements
- autonomous work selection and reselection;
- zero-request ingestion without manufactured goals;
- recursive delegation with bounded authority;
- capability composition and noncommutation handling;
- execution binding and post-state evidence;
- verification and failed-verification reentry;
- persistent cumulative state;
- mathematical map anti-loss capture;
- downstream propagation;
- capability-cut diagnosis versus binding/authority/resource/evidence failure;
- capability acquisition where licensed;
- recovery of old organization behavior: messy corpus -> recover -> organize -> diagnose -> transform -> verify -> reenter;
- preservation of OPEN/INCOMPARABLE and exact provenance/currentness;
- no self-promotion.

## Holdout
At least one unfamiliar frozen task not used to derive the candidate architecture.

## Promotion gate
No production authority transfer until required protected behavior has verified witnesses, blocking regressions are closed or explicitly non-blocking, migration closure is established, and an independent authority transition approves replacement.

No global scalar score. Preserve incomparable outcomes.
