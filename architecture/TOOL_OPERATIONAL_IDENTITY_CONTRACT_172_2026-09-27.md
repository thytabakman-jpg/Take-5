## Authority-route rule

A dimension may be represented in either of two ways:

1. an explicit current value; or
2. a typed route to the canonical owner of that value.

A routed value is not a missing value when the route resolves to a recovered
current authority and the strong tool-reality audit verifies that authority.

A route is OPEN when:
- its target is absent;
- its target is generic-only where the job requires native semantics;
- currentness is unresolved;
- runtime realization is missing;
- the route depends only on remembered chat meaning;
- multiple mutable owners conflict.

The audit never fills a missing coordinate from lexical similarity or from the
tool name alone.
