# Take Two Operating Law 099

Date: 2026-09-26
Status: PROTECTED BEHAVIOR CONTRACT

## What was valuable about Take Two

Take Two was not valuable because it had fewer files.

Its strict-gain behavior was a compact operating law:

OBSERVE
-> FREEZE / TYPE
-> DISCOVER / CHALLENGE
-> DIAGNOSE when diagnosis is the live job
-> DECIDE under authority
-> TRANSITION
-> VERIFY
-> OBSERVE
-> REENTER on material change.

Its five durable semantic primitives were:

Object
Relation
Event
Transition
Observation.

Its architectural laws were:

1. distinctions do not imply separate operating surfaces;
2. consequential change passes through typed Transition;
3. observation precedes causal claims;
4. evidence does not self-authorize;
5. OPEN and incomparability survive compression;
6. migrate capabilities, not files;
7. views/compositions precede new registries or subsystems;
8. architecture is admitted only when composition cannot preserve required semantics;
9. material change forces renewed observation and possible reentry;
10. PD/discovery changes what is known or licensed but does not own mutation authority.

## How it belongs in Take Five

Take Two is a kernel discipline, not a competing current repository.

ImprovementCore supplies adaptive endogenous continuation above this law.

Jane supplies continuity/currentness supervision around it.

Take Five supplies execution truth, provenance, authority, admission, closure, and recovery.

Therefore the protected composition is:

Take-Two kernel law
+ ImproveCore endogenous continuation
+ Jane continuity
+ Take-Five execution/recovery safeguards.

## Anti-regression test

Any successor that requires a new durable surface for every recovered distinction, permits
evidence to mutate state directly, or cannot reobserve after material change fails the
Take-Two preservation test even when all historical Take-Two filenames are gone.

## Friend-facing consequence

A transferable clean system must expose the universal roles rather than personal names:

Kernel
Supervisor
Controller
Capability repertoire
State/evidence/admission/recovery.

Jane and ImproveCore remain valid installation-level configured names.
