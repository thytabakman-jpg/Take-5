# Lessons Ledger

## L001
A remembered tool label can preserve a real behavioral preference even when formal identity is lost.

## L002
Effectiveness can be distributed across routing, completion, reality separation and stewardship rather
than residing in one primitive operator.

## L003
"OPEN" is not one state. Live internal work and certified terminal boundaries require different
controller behavior.

## L004
State-relative selection explains much of the perceived speed/clarity advantage: the system works
on the live distinction rather than replaying a broad fixed lifecycle.

## L005
Execution truth and authority separation prevent backend/runtime failures from being mistaken for
missing mathematics.
