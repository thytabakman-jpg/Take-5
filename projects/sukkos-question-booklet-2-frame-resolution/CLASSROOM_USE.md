# Classroom Use

Status: CURRENT

Core materials:
booklet and pencil.

Adult may:
- invite students to read,
- ask what they notice,
- clarify instructions,
- manage time.

Adult does not supply:
- the hidden assumption on Page 1,
- the three coordinates on Page 2,
- the Page 3 diagnosis,
- the Page 4 rewritten question.

Key observations:
- Does the learner identify a hidden assumption before answering?
- Can the learner say what the question leaves open?
- Can the learner state what would settle the issue?
- Can the learner repair Page 4 without a Sukkos scaffold?
