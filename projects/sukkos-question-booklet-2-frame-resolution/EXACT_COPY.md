## WHAT IS HIDING INSIDE THE QUESTION?

A robot chooses a path each day.

MONDAY
BLUE PATH

TUESDAY
BLUE PATH

WEDNESDAY
BLUE PATH

Someone asks

"Why did the robot stop taking the RED path?"

Can you answer that question?

Before you answer WHY, check what the question already treats as true.

It assumes

The robot used the red path and then stopped.

But the log does not show that.

A question can carry a claim before the answer even begins.

### OUR QUESTION CHECK

ASSUMES

What is the question already treating as true?

OPENS

What possibilities does the question still allow?

SETTLES

What information would count as enough to answer?

QUESTION = ASSUMES + OPENS + SETTLES

Try it

"Why is Route A faster?"

ASSUMES

Route A is faster.

OPENS

Why it is faster.

SETTLES

We first need the actual travel times.

A stronger first question is

"Which route is faster today?"

Pirkei Avot gives the habit.
ASSUMES, OPENS, and SETTLES are our way to inspect the question.

## A QUESTION KOHELET CALLS UNWISE

On Sukkos, Ashkenazi communities traditionally read Kohelet.

Kohelet asks us not to say

"Why were the earlier days better than these?"

Kohelet says this question is not asked from wisdom.

Run our question check.

ASSUMES

The earlier days were better.

OPENS

Why they were better.

It does not leave open whether they were better.

SETTLES

What does "better" mean here?
What evidence would let us compare?

REPAIR IT

Were earlier days better than now in one specific way?
What evidence would let us compare them?

Kohelet calls the question unwise.
Our model is one way to inspect its wording.
