# Project Charter

Status: CURRENT
Date: 2026-09-27

## Purpose

Create a four-page Sukkos booklet that helps a Grade 5-6 learner see that a question can quietly take something for granted, keep some possibilities open, and establish what would count as settling the issue.

## Governing learner change

At the end, a learner can inspect a new question by identifying:
- what it assumes,
- what possibilities it leaves open,
- what evidence would settle it,
and can rewrite a question that smuggles in a disputed assumption.

## Holiday requirement

Sukkos contributes a holiday-native textual case through Kohelet 7:10 and the Ashkenazi custom of reading Kohelet on Sukkot recorded by Rema, Orach Chayim 490:9.

The project does not claim that Kohelet teaches modern formal semantics.

## Form

Exactly four public student pages.
One major learner move per page.
No hidden facilitator-only conceptual bridge.
