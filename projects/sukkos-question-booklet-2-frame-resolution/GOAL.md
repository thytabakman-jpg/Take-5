# Goal

Status: CURRENT

G = <X,T,I,Sigma>

X
Grade 5-6 learner who already asks ordinary questions.

T
Move from treating a question as neutral wording to seeing that a question frames an issue through assumptions, live alternatives, and a resolution condition.

I
A four-page Sukkos learning experience with direct student operations.

Sigma
On an unfamiliar transfer case, the learner identifies a built-in assumption, keeps the relevant alternatives open, states what would settle the issue, and rewrites the question.
