# Four-Page Architecture

Status: ROUTE LOCKED
Date: 2026-09-27

## Whole movement

question-looking sentence
-> discover hidden claim
-> learn a three-part inspection operation
-> use it on a Sukkos-native Kohelet case
-> independently repair a new question.

## Page 1

Title:
WHAT IS HIDING INSIDE THE QUESTION?

Job:
make hidden presupposition visible before terminology.

Encounter:
robot path log shows BLUE every day.

Question:
Why did the robot stop taking the RED path?

Learner move:
notice that the question treats an unsupported event as already true.

Exit:
a question can carry a claim before the answer begins.

Bridge:
How can we inspect a question before we answer it?

## Page 2

Title:
ASK ACCORDING TO THE MATTER

Job:
give the learner the inspection operation.

Jewish anchor:
Pirkei Avot 5:7, שואל כענין.

Model:
ASSUMES / OPENS / SETTLES.

Mini-test:
Why is Route A faster?

Exit:
learner can identify the built-in assumption, the narrowed issue, and what evidence is needed.

Bridge:
Can Sukkos give us a question that looks normal but closes something too early?

## Page 3

Title:
A QUESTION KOHELET CALLS UNWISE

Job:
provide a Sukkos-native canonical question for inspection.

Holiday reveal:
Rema records the custom of reading Kohelet on Sukkot.

Kohelet case:
Why were the earlier days better than these?

Learner move:
apply ASSUMES / OPENS / SETTLES and repair the question.

Boundary:
Kohelet supplies the question and wisdom judgment.
The formal diagnosis is our educational application.

Exit:
learner sees that wise questioning includes inspecting the frame.

Bridge:
Can you catch the same problem in a totally different subject?

## Page 4

Title:
REBUILD THE QUESTION

Job:
independent transfer.

Case:
paper bridge lab before testing.

Question:
Why is Bridge A stronger than Bridge B?

Learner move:
identify assumption, reopen both outcomes, define strength, rewrite the question.

Exit:
learner independently constructs a question that does not force the answer and has a resolution test.
