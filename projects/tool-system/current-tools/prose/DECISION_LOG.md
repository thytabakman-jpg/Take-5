# Decision log: Prose

Append-only.

## 2026-09-30 D001

Admitted Prose as a distinct configured tool after RootCause HF2 showed that
concrete reader-facing prose acceptance is result-sensitive and was not owned by
Architecture, SolutionToMyProblem, or generic verification.

## 2026-09-30 D002

Historical dating in reader-facing prose uses Arabic year numbers. Century-label
dating such as "12th century" or "twelfth century" is prohibited. Uncertainty
remains expressible with numeric forms such as "c. 1075–1141", "fl. 1170–1190",
and "d. 1204". Unknown dates stay OPEN rather than being guessed.
