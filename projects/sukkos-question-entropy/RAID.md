# RAID

> Governing authority: GOAL.md. This file may specialize the governing goal but may not override it.

## Risks

R1
Entropy becomes a decorative "cool equation" unrelated to Page 2.
Mitigation: Page 2 must explicitly operationalize uncertainty reduction.

R2
The four-species 2x2 is presented as if the Midrash were doing probability theory.
Mitigation: source/application boundary is mandatory.

R3
Taste/smell is treated as literal empirical classification of the ritual objects.
Mitigation: state that the Midrash supplies a symbolic analogy; do not stage a sensory lab.

R4
WH-question anatomy is falsely presented as universal.
Mitigation: teach it as one high-value canonical information-question scaffold.

R5
"Bind them together" becomes generic unity messaging and loses the questioning lesson.
Mitigation: Page 3 must preserve the sequence distinguish accurately -> then reintegrate.

R6
Page 4 becomes reflective rather than performative.
Mitigation: independent question generation is required evidence.

## Assumptions

- Grade 5-6 can work with a simplified finite possibility model.
- Exact probability notation can be visually scaffolded without teaching probability theory in full.
