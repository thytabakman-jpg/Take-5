> Governing authority: GOAL.md. This file may specialize the governing goal but may not override it.

Status: CLOSED_RELATIVE / GOVERNING
Date: 2026-09-27

G = <X,T,I,Sigma>

X
Grade 5-6 learner in a one-session Sukkos lesson. A salient use case is a learner who understands questions but struggles to generate them.

T
Move from experiencing uncertainty as an undifferentiated state to treating it as something that can be reduced through deliberately constructed questions.

I
A four-page sequence:
real mathematical structure -> human question technology -> Sukkos religious surplus -> independent transfer.

Sigma
On a new case with multiple live possibilities, the learner identifies what is unknown, constructs a usable information-seeking question, and explains how the answer would reduce the live possibility space.

Protected constraints
- Page 1 uses real established mathematics, not invented pseudo-math.
- Page 2 contains a usable human technology before the Sukkos reveal.
- Page 3 is Sukkos-native and adds genuine surplus.
- Page 4 demonstrates independent transfer.
- The canonical WH-question scaffold is not misrepresented as a universal grammar of all questions.
- Emotional depth concentrates on agency in Pages 2 and 4.
