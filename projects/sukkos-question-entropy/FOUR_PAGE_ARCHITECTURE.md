# Four-Page Architecture

Status: LOCKED FOR RENDER CANDIDATE

## Whole movement

MEASURE THE UNKNOWN
→ ASK TO CHANGE WHAT REMAINS
→ DISTINGUISH WITHOUT SEPARATING
→ ASK INSTEAD OF GUESSING

## Page 1 — HOW MUCH DON'T WE KNOW?

Job:
make uncertainty visible and measurable.

Visual:
eight-card possibility field.

Move:
8 → 4 → 2 → 1 and 3 → 2 → 1 → 0 bits.

Exit:
an answer can reduce what remains possible.

Bottom handoff:
"If uncertainty can get smaller, what can we do to make it smaller?"

## Page 2 — ASK TO THE POINT

Job:
turn uncertainty reduction into a human/Jewish question technology.

Visual:
one large cloud of possibilities; one question cuts it into two answer paths.

Source ribbon:
שואל כענין — ask according to the matter.

Construction scaffold:
For many information questions:
question word + helper verb + subject + main verb.
For yes/no questions, the helper verb can come first.

Exit:
a question works when its possible answers change the live possibility set.

Bottom handoff:
"Can Sukkos show us how to notice real differences without turning them into separation?"

## Page 3 — FOUR DIFFERENCES. ONE GROUP.

Job:
Sukkos-native embodiment.

Visual:
2×2 taste/smell matrix with the four species.
Then the differentiated four visually become one mitzvah set.

Exit:
distinction can clarify without requiring separation.

Bottom handoff:
"When I do not know, can I use a question instead of filling the gap with a guess?"

## Page 4 — DON'T GUESS. ASK.

Job:
independent transfer with emotional payoff.

Scene:
At recess, a friend is missing from the usual meeting spot.
Four live possibilities are pictured:
library, office, gym, absent.
A classmate has just come from inside.

Learner task:
write one question in one oversized speech bubble that would remove possibilities.

No boxes.
No multi-field worksheet.
No forced personal disclosure.

Closing:
"You do not have to know yet. You can ask the next question."
