# Visual Master

Status: LOCKED FOR RENDER CANDIDATE

## House style

Warm cream ground.
Elegant hand-drawn educational illustration.
Strong charcoal typography.
Restrained warm accents.
No photorealism.
No blue-gray cards.
No corporate infographic style.
No clutter.
Small deer logo in a bottom corner on every page.

The visual carries the argument before the text is read.

## Page 1

Hero:
2×4 grid of eight playful abstract cards.
Attributes use shape, pattern, and symbol, never color alone.

Three large question sweeps progressively dim eliminated cards.
8→4→2→1 and 3→2→1→0 sit directly in the visual path.

Formula prominent but secondary to transformation.

## Page 2

Hero:
cloud of live possibilities split by one large question-shaped gate into YES and NO paths.

Pirkei Avot phrase appears as a small warm parchment ribbon, never a portrait.

Question construction appears as one connected sentence-strip, not four boxes.

## Page 3

Hero:
large 2×2 matrix.
Axes are TASTE and SMELL.
Each quadrant contains one illustrated species.

Below, four paths curve inward to one hand-held four-species composition.

Visual sentence:
difference first, one group second.

## Page 4

Hero:
full-page illustrated school/recess scene.
Four possible-location mini-scenes float like thought bubbles around one central learner.
One oversized speech bubble is the only writing space.

The page feels like a story ending, not an assignment sheet.

## Continuity

Page 1 many possibilities.
Page 2 one question cuts the space.
Page 3 distinctions form a pattern and then rejoin.
Page 4 learner owns the next cut.
