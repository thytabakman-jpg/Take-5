## Vayikra Rabbah 30:12

Allowed structure:
The Midrash compares the four species to four human types using paired distinctions of taste/no taste and smell/no smell, then says they become one grouping.

Allowed abstraction:
two binary distinctions generate four symbolic combinations.

Not allowed:
presenting taste and smell as halachic tests for identifying species;
presenting the Midrashic human typology as a scientific taxonomy;
claiming the etrog is ordinarily tied into the lulav bundle.
