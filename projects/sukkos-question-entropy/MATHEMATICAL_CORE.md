Canonical quantity: Shannon entropy.

H(X) = - sum_x p(x) log_2 p(x)

Use: quantify uncertainty / expected information in a random variable.
Balanced special case: N equally likely possibilities gives H(X) = log_2 N.

Page-1 permission: compare finite possibility spaces.
Forbidden: "entropy is the equation for a question."

Bridge: information revealed by an answer can reduce remaining uncertainty.
Boundary: literal information gain from an everyday question requires a probabilistic model; the booklet uses deliberately simple finite cases.
