# Classroom Use

Status: READY FOR PILOT / EMPIRICAL VALIDATION OPEN

## Materials

One booklet per learner.
Pencil.

## Movement

Page 1:
students predict which cards remain after each answer.

Page 2:
students offer a question that removes at least one live possibility; read שואל כענין.

Page 3:
students place the species in the taste/smell matrix, then discuss the Midrash's move from four types to one group.

Page 4:
students independently author one question in the speech bubble, then optional sharing.

## Facilitation boundary

Teacher can read, pace, and ask learners to explain reasoning.

Teacher does not supply the Page 4 question.

## Observe

Do learners understand entropy here as uncertainty rather than disorder?
Do they notice that an uninformative question can leave every possibility alive?
Does Page 3 remain source-accurate?
Do they generate Page 4 questions independently?
Does Page 4 feel like a story rather than a worksheet?
