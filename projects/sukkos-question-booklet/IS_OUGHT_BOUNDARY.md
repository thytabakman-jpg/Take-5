# Is/Ought and Cross-Domain Boundary

Status: CURRENT PROTECTION

## Distinction

The mathematical model describes structural features of questions.

It does not prove that a person is morally or religiously obligated to ask questions, ask a particular question, value curiosity, or accept a Jewish educational norm.

Therefore:

descriptive question structure
does not entail
educational or Jewish value.

## Licensed bridge

1. Mathematics or logic makes a structure inspectable.
2. The learner encounters a human problem or need that the structure exposes.
3. Jewish material supplies a value, practice, distinction, or mode of inquiry that addresses that human problem.

Sukkos then embodies or deepens that Jewish operation.

## Prohibited collapse

Do not write math says X, therefore Judaism says X.
Do not write a question narrows possibilities, therefore people are obligated to question everything.
Do not treat a scientific or mathematical term as evidence for a human norm merely because the words sound similar.
