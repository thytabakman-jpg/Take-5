# Educational Philosophy

Status: WORKING

## Central view

A question is not merely punctuation and not merely a sentence beginning with who, what, when, where, why, or how.

A question is an organized information gap.

Learning to ask better questions means learning to notice what is unknown, what is already being assumed or supplied, what kinds of answers are possible, and what would resolve the uncertainty.

## Inquiry

notice
→ identify the unknown
→ identify relevant givens or constraints
→ ask
→ test an answer against the resolution condition
→ update the question when the answer exposes a new gap.

## Developmental compression

Backend precision can exceed student-facing precision.

The booklet only exposes distinctions that materially improve the learner's ability to ask or repair a question.

## Jewish layer

The Jewish contribution cannot be "Judaism likes questions" as a decorative slogan.

It needs an operative discipline that changes how the learner asks, answers, attends, distinguishes, or investigates.

## Holiday layer

Sukkos cannot merely provide a seasonal example.

The physical, legal, ritual, temporal, or communal structure of Sukkos must make the question operation newly usable or visible.
