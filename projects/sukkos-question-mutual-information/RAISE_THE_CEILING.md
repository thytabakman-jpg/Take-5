Page 2
Do not turn target selection into abstract vocabulary. Give the learner a real reason to care which clue/question is useful. The emotional turn is from "I don't know what to ask" to a visible target that generates a route.

Page 4
Do not reward grammatical correctness alone. The learner must choose a question because its possible answers matter for the target. Let the success be felt before naming it.

Page 3
Preserve the source's tension: meaningful distinctions exist, yet the differentiated types are joined. Avoid generic unity messaging and avoid implying categories exhaust a person.

Global deep idea
A question becomes powerful when it is connected to what you are actually trying to know; a useful distinction can reveal without reducing the whole to the distinction.
