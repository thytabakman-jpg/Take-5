# Project Charter
> Governing authority: GOAL.md. This file may specialize the governing goal but may not override it.

Purpose: create a four-page Sukkos booklet that begins with real mathematics of target-relative information, turns that into a usable human question-selection/construction technology, shows a Sukkos-native religious surplus, and ends with independent transfer.

Learner change: identify what they are trying to find out, choose a distinction whose answer would actually inform that target, construct the question, and use the answer to update what they know.

Holiday requirement: Page 3 must add more than an example. Current route uses Leviticus 23:40 plus Vayikra Rabbah 30:12: a pair of distinctions differentiates symbolic types, followed by binding differentiated types into one grouping.

Form: exactly four student pages; one major job per page; strongest emotional agency on Pages 2 and 4; no required personal disclosure.
