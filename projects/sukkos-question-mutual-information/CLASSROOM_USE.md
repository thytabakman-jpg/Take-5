# Classroom Use

Status: READY FOR PILOT / EMPIRICAL VALIDATION OPEN

## Materials

One booklet per learner.
Pencil.

## Movement

Page 1:
students compare what STRIPED? tells them versus PAPER?.

Page 2:
students name a target first, then propose one question aimed at it; read שואל כענין.

Page 3:
students identify which distinctions matter for locating a species in the Midrash's taste/smell map, then discuss the move to one group.

Page 4:
students independently write one target-relevant question in the speech bubble.

## Facilitation boundary

Teacher can pace, read, and invite explanations.

Teacher does not supply the Page 4 question.

## Observe

Do learners distinguish interesting information from target-relevant information?
Do they identify the target before constructing a question?
Does Page 3 remain clearly Midrashic rather than halachic taxonomy?
Can they author the Page 4 question independently?
Does Page 4 feel like a story rather than a form?
