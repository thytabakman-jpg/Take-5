# Classroom Use
> Governing authority: GOAL.md. This file may specialize the governing goal but may not override it.

Adult may invite observation, read source text, clarify vocabulary, ask bridge questions, manage time.

Adult does not choose the learner's Page-2 target distinction, reveal the Page-3 conclusion before inspection, or supply the Page-4 question.

Primary observation: can learner name what they are trying to find out and build a relevant question?
Secondary: can learner explain how possible answers affect the target?
