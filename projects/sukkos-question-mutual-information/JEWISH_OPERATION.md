# Jewish Inquiry Operation

Status: LOCKED_RELATIVE

## Source

Pirkei Avot 5:7:

שואל כענין

Student rendering:
"Ask according to the matter."

## Function

The source does not teach mutual information.

It supplies a Jewish inquiry discipline that fits this route closely:
questions are asked in relation to the matter at hand.

Page 2 therefore begins with:
"What am I trying to know?"

Then:
"What answer would actually tell me something about that?"

## Placement

Page 2, before Sukkos appears.

## Boundary

Mathematics describes dependence between variables.
Pirkei Avot contributes an inquiry norm.
Neither is used as proof of the other.
