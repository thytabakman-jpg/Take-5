# Visual Master

Status: LOCKED FOR RENDER CANDIDATE

## House style

Warm cream ground.
Elegant illustrated educational nonfiction.
Hand-drawn vector/gouache feel.
Charcoal typography.
Restrained warm accent colors.
No photorealism.
No blue-gray UI boxes.
No corporate slide aesthetic.
Small deer logo in a bottom corner.

The visual carries relevance versus irrelevance before the text explains it.

## Page 1

Hero:
eight-card field with a glowing target ring around the set.

Two large question paths:
STRIPED? sends cards into two meaningful branches.
PAPER? loops back to the unchanged full set.

The equation sits between target and questions.

## Page 2

Hero:
large bullseye/target in the center.

Several information arrows approach.
One or two connect to the target.
Others visibly miss or pass behind.

Pirkei Avot phrase sits on a warm source ribbon.

Question grammar is a single build-strip along the bottom third, not boxed fields.

## Page 3

Hero:
large taste/smell 2×2 matrix.
A visible target badge says:
"Which place in this Midrashic map?"

The four species occupy the quadrants.
Below, their paths curve into one shared hand-held composition.

## Page 4

Hero:
one cinematic school-dismissal scene.
The learner's target appears as a single strong sign:
"Does my classmate know the pickup time?"

Irrelevant details are present visually but subdued.
One oversized speech bubble is the only writable element.

This page must feel like clarity emerging from noise, not like a worksheet.
