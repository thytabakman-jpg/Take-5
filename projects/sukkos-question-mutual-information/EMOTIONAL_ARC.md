# Emotional Arc
> Governing authority: GOAL.md. This file may specialize the governing goal but may not override it.

Status: RAISED CEILING / BACKEND-COMPATIBLE

P1 Wonder: information is relational; a fact can matter enormously for one target and almost not at all for another.

P2 Primary beat: direction. The learner begins with "What am I trying to find out?" and experiences that a question does not have to appear whole; the target helps generate it. Preferred later-copy shape: "Name what you want to know. Then build the question that can move you toward it."

P3 Secondary beat: being described by a distinction is not the same as being reduced to that distinction. The source differentiates and then binds together.

P4 Primary beat: earned agency. The learner selects the target and builds the useful question without a provided template answer. Preferred later-copy shape: "I can choose what I need to know next."

No forced disclosure or therapeutic framing.
