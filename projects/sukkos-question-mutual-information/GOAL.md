# Goal

Status: CLOSED_RELATIVE / GOVERNING

G = <X,T,I,Sigma>

X
Grade 5-6 learner in a one-session Sukkos lesson; high-value case includes a learner who understands questions but struggles to generate one.

T
Move from "I need information" without a route to target-directed question construction: identify the target, identify a potentially informative distinction, build the question, and use the answer.

I
real mathematical structure -> human target/question technology -> Sukkos religious surplus -> independent transfer.

Sigma
On a new case, the learner names what they are trying to find out, chooses a distinction relevant to that target, constructs a question, and explains how different answers would change what they know about the target.

Protected constraints
- Page 1 uses established mutual-information mathematics;
- Page 2 is usable before Sukkos;
- Page 3 is Sukkos-native and adds genuine surplus;
- Page 4 independently demonstrates target-directed question generation;
- WH scaffold is not universalized;
- emotion is carried mainly by direction/agency on Pages 2 and 4.
