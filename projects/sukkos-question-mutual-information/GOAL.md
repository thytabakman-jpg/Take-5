# Goal

Status: CURRENT

G = <X,T,I,Sigma>

X
A four-page Grade 5–6 Sukkos booklet built on mutual information and target-relative question usefulness.

T
A render-ready artifact in which the learner names what they are trying to know, distinguishes relevant from irrelevant information, and builds one question whose answer changes what they know about that target.

I
Complete route, copy, sources, visuals, render constraints, classroom mechanics, and verification while preserving a genuinely different cognitive job from the entropy booklet.

Sigma
No hidden teacher bridge.
No claim that all information is equally useful.
No equation-as-decoration.
No claim that taste/smell halachically identifies the four species.
No Page 4 worksheet grid.
No photorealism.
No renderer-authored curriculum.
