# PAGE 3

## WHICH DIFFERENCES MATTER HERE?

Vayikra Rabbah looks at the four species through two differences.

TASTE

SMELL

Those two differences make a four-part map.

ETROG
taste + smell

DATE PALM
taste + no smell

MYRTLE
no taste + smell

WILLOW
no taste + no smell

If the target is

WHERE DOES THIS SPECIES FIT IN THE MIDRASH'S MAP?

taste and smell matter.

But the Midrash does not stop with four separate types.

It says to make them

ONE GROUP

Vayikra Rabbah 30:12

The right difference helps us understand the parts.

It does not require us to split the whole.

When I need to know one thing, can I ask about that instead of everything around it?
