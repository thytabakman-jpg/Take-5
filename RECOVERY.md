# Recovery

Use this order when the system appears downgraded, compressed, or partially lost.

1. Recover the current target, job, authority/basis, and canonical version.
2. Load the protected behavior set.
3. Load known OPEN/BLOCKED/CONFLICT coordinates.
4. Validate the capability-preservation manifest.
5. Reconstruct Supervisor continuity context.
6. Reconstruct Controller state and learning memory.
7. Confirm inquiry/work generation precedes capability selection.
8. Confirm execution/admission/persistence/consumption are distinct.
9. Confirm consequential mutations pass the Kernel transition membrane.
10. Compare any proposed successor against preservation witnesses.
11. Reenter after material representation, evidence, dependency, authority, or state change.
12. Terminate only with typed relative closure.

Do not recover from names alone.
Do not infer currentness from newest timestamp.
Do not use last-writer-wins across concurrent work.
