# load-bearing

Object ID: TERM:LOAD_BEARING
Type: SEMANTIC_PREDICATE

## Current definition

A term/object is load-bearing for a job when changing or misresolving it can change a protected result, legal continuation, routing, authority, verification, preservation, or closure. Exact universal admission mathematics remains open.

## Known

- result sensitivity is sufficient evidence of load-bearingness
- uncertain load-bearingness defaults to capture rather than omission
- ordinary non-result-sensitive wording may be exempt
