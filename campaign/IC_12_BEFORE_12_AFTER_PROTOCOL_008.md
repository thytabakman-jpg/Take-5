## Mandatory MTA preflight
MTA runs before every material Improvement Core episode.

MTA result on this protocol:
The user's concern is not lack of named capabilities. It is unreliable activation. A behavior that exists but is not selected cannot improve the episode.

Therefore add an episode-level activation contract, not another capability.

For episode e:
Pre(e) = MTA + 12 independently selected lenses.
Execute(e) = the material job selected after consuming Pre.
Post(e) = 12 independently selected lenses on the actual output/evidence.
Reenter(e) = consume material post deltas before closure/next episode.

A lens counts only when it returns a receipt:
<lens,target,observation,material_delta,disposition,consumed>.

NO_DELTA is valid. Mentioning a lens is not use.
