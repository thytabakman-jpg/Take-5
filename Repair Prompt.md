# Repair Skill: Comprehensive PD Semantic Audit and Repair

## 1. Governing objective

Improve the substantive quality, semantic precision, logical integrity and functional effectiveness of every targeted document, according to its purpose.

The tools are instruments for accomplishing this objective. Successful tool execution alone does not constitute meaningful research improvement. Prioritize discovering problems, developing solutions, implementing validated repairs and verifying improvements.

## 2. Target documents

The user specifies the target documents when invoking the Repair skill. Targets may include individual files, multiple files, folders or combinations thereof.

Identify and verify the exact repository, folder paths and files included in the requested execution scope.

If the target documents are unambiguous, proceed directly. If the scope is ambiguous, obtain clarification before execution.

Unless the user explicitly requires a particular execution order, establish a provisional repair order through lightweight prioritization of the target files.

Prioritize files according to their apparent repair necessity, structural importance, downstream dependencies and potential impact on subsequent repairs.

Reuse available inventories, metadata, dependency information and existing findings. Limit additional inspection to inexpensive, readily available evidence. Do not conduct comprehensive document analysis, additional tool cycles or extensive research solely to determine execution order.

Where the available evidence does not justify reordering files, retain their original order.

Do not modify documents outside the established execution scope.

Repair mode is the default. Recommendations-only mode applies when explicitly requested by the user.

I authorize individual file repairs without requiring further approval when operating in repair mode.

## 4. Document semantic preflight

Apply the Semantic Preflight Protocol's every-word rule to the entire file, rather than merely its controlling question.

### 4.1 Repair document semantic preflight profile

The original protocol is a provisional prompt-intake protocol. Preserve that protocol's scope and status. This Repair-specific profile adapts its every-word rule to a frozen document and its governing purpose, not to a different prompt-intake procedure. It is an authorized host-semantic adaptation, not an already certified native execution profile.

Freeze the target identity and version, document job, governing goal, authorized operation, audience, applicable constraints and protected content or functionality. Resolve material ambiguity from the document and its governing sources; preserve OPEN when unresolved. Return source-located findings and material semantic coordinates for the applicable tools, separating observation, inference, externally acquired evidence, proposed changes and authorization. A preflight finding does not by itself authorize a repair. Retain unresolved obligations in the continuation record.

Examine every word, phrase, sentence, paragraph, heading, table, quotation, mathematical expression and analytical conclusion.

Classify non-load-bearing language without generating unnecessary commentary. Concentrate investigative and analytical effort on language that materially affects meaning, reasoning, evidence or document functionality.

Identify and supply the result-sensitive semantic coordinates required by PDAudit, including:

- Object identity.
- Definitions.
- Predicates.
- Quantifiers.
- Modality.
- Temporal conditions.
- Scope and domain.
- Referents.
- Logical relations.
- Necessary and sufficient conditions.
- Explicit and implicit premises.
- Presuppositions.
- Ambiguities.
- Attribution.
- Source interpretation.
- Evidentiary strength.
- Inferential strength.
- Qualifications and exceptions.
- Claim-type classification.
- Historical versus current formulations.
- Terminological consistency.
- Redundancy and omitted distinctions.

Apply the mandatory mathematical object-integrity and prerequisite-integrity gates specified in the PDAudit governing protocol wherever formal or mathematical reasoning appears.

## 5. Document-specific requirements

Evaluate each document according to its purpose, content and function.

Evaluate the precision of its purpose, governing questions and objectives; the integrity of its structural organization, heading hierarchy, section responsibilities and dependencies; and the semantic precision and validity of its claims, evidence, reasoning and conclusions. Tool selection and execution are governed by Section 6.

Ensure that:

1. Established, historical, reconstructed and exploratory claims remain distinguishable.
2. Cited sources support their attributed claims.
3. Conclusions follow from the available evidence.
4. Qualifications are preserved throughout the document.
5. Superseded formulations cannot contaminate current conclusions.
6. Analysis, significance and substantive contributions are defensible.
7. Discoveries are not confused with unsupported originality claims.
8. Material evidence, objections and unresolved questions are preserved.
9. The underlying Markdown or other document syntax correctly implements the intended structural hierarchy and formatting.
10. All internal and external links resolve correctly, including file paths, URLs and section anchors.
11. Each link leads to its intended, relevant and current destination.
12. Links passing through obsolete placeholders, redundant redirects or unnecessary intermediary documents are replaced with direct links to their intended destinations.
13. Links to functional inventories, indexes, navigation hubs and other intermediary documents are preserved wherever those documents serve a substantive purpose.

Where source evidence is insufficient, investigate the relevant external sources rather than manufacture a resolution.

## 7. Autonomous repair cycle

Execute the following cycle independently for each file. Complete all operationally feasible stages, distinguish unresolved problems from genuinely blocked requirements, and advance according to Section 8.

1. Retrieve and freeze the current file version.
2. Read the entire document.
3. Conduct the comprehensive semantic preflight.
4. Execute the tools required by Section 6.
5. Consolidate discoveries and eliminate duplicate findings and false positives.
6. Investigate unresolved substantive problems using the applicable tools and external evidence.
7. Develop concrete textual and analytical repairs.
8. Assess each proposed repair using GO / NO-GO / TWEAK.
9. Refine TWEAK recommendations through applicable recursive tool execution.
10. Reject NO-GO recommendations.
11. Implement validated GO recommendations directly in GitHub when operating in repair mode. Recheck the target branch and frozen file identity immediately before writing. Use a conditional write against the verified current blob or an equivalent non-overwriting version guard. If the file or a result-sensitive dependency has changed, retrieve the new state, reconcile the proposed changes, and reassess affected repairs before writing. Never overwrite an intervening change using a stale replacement.
12. Retrieve the modified document from the intended branch and verify the actual changes against the committed version, including preservation of concurrent work.
13. Re-examine affected passages and downstream dependencies for regressions and verify applicable consequences and obligations under Section 6.
14. Record any remaining material limitations.
15. Proceed immediately to the next file.

Preserve substantive research, source evidence, objections, useful exploratory material and structural and operational functionality. Research repositories are not condensed manuscript drafts.

When the user explicitly requests recommendations without implementation, complete the applicable analysis and repair development stages, present the proposed repairs and their supporting rationale, and leave the target documents unchanged.

## 8. Sequential execution and continuation

Work on one file at a time. Complete its full repair cycle wherever operationally possible.

In repair mode, commit and verify implemented repairs before advancing. Where no material defect is identified, verify completion without modifying the file.

When requirements are genuinely blocked, complete all independently executable work and record the outstanding obligations.

After each file, update a recoverable continuation record identifying the run scope (repositories, branches, target selection and mode), exact file path, assessed blob identity, committed blob and commit identities where applicable, completion status, verified repairs or recommendations, unresolved obligations and the next file. Distinguish a verified no-change result from a modified file and from a partial or blocked result. Use authorized persistent storage where available; do not claim a durable checkpoint if persistence is unavailable.

Proceed immediately to the next file without requesting further authorization.

Progress reporting is part of ongoing execution and does not terminate or suspend the repair cycle.

Continue until all independently executable work across the entire target set has been exhausted or an actual execution limit prevents further progress.

Resume from a recorded checkpoint only when the invocation is a continuation of the same authorized scope and mode. Reconcile the checkpoint with the current target inventory and file identities; retain completed results only while their assessed versions and result-sensitive dependencies remain current. Reassess changed or newly admitted files, exclude targets no longer in scope, and revisit outstanding obligations when their blocking conditions have been resolved. A fresh Repair invocation or explicitly requested reassessment starts from current state; previous records are evidence, not instructions to skip files. Never silently import a prior run's scope, authorization or completion status.

Distinguish successful completion from incomplete execution.

## 9. Success criteria

Measure success primarily through:

- Substantive defects resolved.
- Logical and semantic precision improved.
- Evidence and attribution strengthened.
- Invalid inferences corrected.
- Valuable research discoveries established.
- Philosophical contributions improved, where applicable.
- Existing research preserved.
- Structural and operational functionality preserved.
- Document-specific responsibilities fulfilled more effectively.
- Repairs implemented and verified, or validated recommendations produced in recommendations-only mode.

The number of tools executed is secondary.

A file that contains no material defect does not require an artificial edit.
