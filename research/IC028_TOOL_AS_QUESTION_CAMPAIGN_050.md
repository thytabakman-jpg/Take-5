## PD

Distinguish:
Q = question/information target.
P = probe/inquiry operation.
T = configured tool/program.
A = answer/evidence returned.
U = admitted state update.

A question can exist without an executable method.
A tool can perform transforms/verification/persistence in addition to posing a question.

Therefore literal identity Tool=Question is too strong.

Stronger candidate:
Every epistemic research tool has an erotetic nucleus.

E(T)=<Q_T,AnswerSpace_T,Presuppositions_T,AdmissibleProbe_T,AnswerAdmission_T,UpdateEffect_T>.

For many meta-tools, the named tool is a configured strategy for asking a characteristic family of questions.

Architect: What roles/relations/interfaces/authority/state are required?

## External question-theory comparison

Established fields already formalize important parts:
- erotetic logic studies questions, answer relations, and inferential relations among questions;
- inferential erotetic logic studies how questions arise from contexts and how questions lead to further questions;
- Hintikka's interrogative model treats inquiry as deriving a target using deductive steps plus answers obtained by questioning an information source/Nature;
- later work treats inquiry as a dynamic procedure and studies strategies of inquiry.

This is a strong external comparator, not proof our architecture is equivalent.
