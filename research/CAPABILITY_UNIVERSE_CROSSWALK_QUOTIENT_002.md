## Important MTA result

The current evidence does NOT support a new monolithic middle "tool."

It supports a compact typed algebra whose shape is strikingly close to the existing system basis:

T=<K,S,O,G,M,C,R,U>

with O={DIFFERENTIATE,RELATE,RECONSTRUCT,STRENGTHEN}

and verification/closure represented through result carrier, C/R obligations and explicit validation programs rather than automatically promoted to a ninth primitive role.

Therefore the new work is converging on refinement/validation of the eight-role algebra, not replacement by a giant meta-tool.
