### Is/Ought interface

Humean gap constrains an inference form:
Descriptive premises alone do not license a normative conclusion without a normative bridge/standard.

REBT demandingness is not identical to the is/ought gap.
Its potentially load-bearing intersection is the status of the bridge from preference/evaluation to necessity/demand.

Candidate distinction:
Preference: I want p.
Normative/goal-relative evaluation: Given standard g, p is preferable/required under g.
Absolutist demand: p must obtain / I or reality cannot acceptably be otherwise.

The first-to-third transition can contain an unlicensed strengthening even when a normative standard exists.

### FACTOR

Three axes survive:
A inferential source: descriptive / normative-bridge / modal-absolutist.
B support: supported-under-declared-standard / unsupported / OPEN.
C rigidity: flexible preference / conditional requirement / absolutist demand.

These are not reducible to one "oughtness" axis.

### Root Cause

Primary conceptual failure:
NORMATIVE-FORCE / MODAL-FORCE / PSYCHOLOGICAL-RIGIDITY COLLAPSE.

Secondary:
STANDARD-SUPPRESSION.
A sentence can look like an unsupported ought only because the governing goal/standard is implicit.

Clinical secondary:
LEVEL-TRANSPORT OVERREACH.
A logical defect in a proposition does not by itself establish a psychological construct or causal role in distress.

The mapping rho is load-bearing and must be sourced/reconstructed from REBT theory; it cannot be invented from Hume.

### Multi-Object interaction

The important interaction is not Hume + REBT = same rule.

It is:
is/ought analysis exposes bridge premises and force changes;
PD exposes hidden standards and object conflation;
REBT supplies a theory-specific classification of rigid evaluative beliefs;
clinical evidence tests whether classified constructs relate causally to distress.

Thus each domain performs a nonredundant job.

### Candidate Universe

H1 Hume directly explains REBT demandingness.
H2 Hume only supplies an audit constraint on normative inference.
H3 shared deeper variable is illicit force escalation from preference/evaluation to necessity.
H4 connection is mostly linguistic and disappears under typing.
H5 REBT rationality is pragmatic/empirical and independent of Humean validity.
H6 mixed model: Hume is load-bearing for bridge/force audit but insufficient for REBT classification.

Current disposition:
H1 unsupported.
H2 survives.
H3 promising, requires exact REBT textual/theoretical reconstruction.
H4 not eliminated.
H5 partly true but cannot replace full REBT mapping without sources.
H6 strongest working synthesis, not final truth claim.

These are prospective tests, not claimed empirical results.
