## Problem

A later, cleaner, compressed, or more mathematical version does not automatically preserve the
full behavior of an earlier tool/configuration.

Names and version numbers do not establish identity or successor status.

## Historical version object

For every recoverable version v, record:

D(v)=<J,N,E,M,Pi,C,U,A>

where:
J = protected job;
N = semantic nucleus/mathematics;
E = execution envelope/wrappers/I-O;
M = mode policy;
Pi = orchestration/order/scope/local freedom;
C = closure/reentry;
U = update/persistence/propagation/external escape;
A = authority/binding/runtime boundary.

Also retain W(v), the demonstrated/protected witness set.

Unknown coordinates remain OPEN.

## Successor admission

A candidate v2 does not replace v1 merely because it is later or compressed.

A preserving-successor claim requires:
1. same protected job or explicit typed job change;
2. reconstruction of D(v1) and D(v2);
3. preservation of every protected witness in W(v1);
4. no regression on matched cases;
5. strict gain or another explicit reason for canonical replacement.

Allowed relation classes:
EQUIVALENT_REWRITE
PRESERVING_SUCCESSOR
FORK_NEW_JOB
ALTERNATE_CONFIGURATION
REGRESSION
PARTIAL_OVERLAP
OPEN.

## Capability identity

Capability labels are not mathematical identity.

A capability is represented by a typed relation/map plus preconditions, protected effects,
state/evidence effects, and continuation behavior.

Collapse requires typed-map equality or declared-basis behavioral equivalence.

Counterexamples block collapse.

## Take-5 integration

This contract extends FUNCTIONALITY_RECOVERY_LEDGER rather than replacing it.

Recovered historical versions enter the artifact-to-work intake/currentness path.

No historical file becomes canonical by copy.

For every recovered version:
- preserve source provenance;
- construct D(v);
- freeze W(v);
- compare to predecessor/candidate successor;
- route unresolved coordinates as OPEN work;
- update current capability/tool identity only after successor admission.

## First protected witness family

Early MT demonstrated:
- whole-object goal-decoupled reconnaissance;
- self-generated local questions;
- no mutation during reconnaissance;
- LOOK -> MODEL -> CHOOSE -> DO -> CHECK -> LOOK AGAIN;
- recursive consequence harvest/persist/verify/propagate/reentry;
- unexpected cross-object/cross-scale discovery.

Later MT may represent these coordinates without proving they execute equivalently.

Therefore:
EARLY_MT -> LATER_MT remains OPEN/PARTIAL_OVERLAP until matched witnesses pass.

## Completion

Historical backfill is not complete when each family has a newest version.

It is complete relative to the recovered corpus when every recoverable version has:
- lineage location;
- protected job;
- D(v) or typed OPEN fields;
- W(v);
- predecessor/successor relation;
- current disposition with grounds.
