## Frozen job

Prevent a newly introduced load-bearing semantic object or relation from silently changing the admitted project state before its identity, type, grounds, authority and unresolved coordinates are explicit.

## Representation attack

The predecessor centered on new named objects. That trigger is too narrow.

A project can change governing meaning when:
- an old object receives a new relation;
- a default or stopping rule changes;
- scope changes;
- old objects are composed differently;
- descriptive material becomes normative;
- a representation change hides a semantic change.

Therefore the more primitive object is SEMANTIC DELTA.

Let S_t be current admitted state and r a proposed result.
Let Delta_JH(S_t,r) be its result-sensitive semantic delta under job J and continuation basis H.

Let GovDelta be the portion capable of changing protected results, authority, interpretation, routing, verification obligations, transitions or completion.

Candidate rule:

nonempty GovDelta
implies
a typed disposition before that delta changes admitted governing state.

Allowed dispositions:
ADMIT
REJECT
MERGE
RIVAL
OPEN
BLOCKED.

OPEN may persist as epistemic state without silently receiving governing force.

## Minimal factorization candidate

SGA = <D,T,A,G>

D Detect Delta
Recover whether the result changes governing semantics.

T Type Delta
Freeze identity and classify the changed object, relation, invariant, policy, scope, authority, representation, transition, evidence status or completion rule.

A Adjudicate Delta
Return typed disposition with grounds and authority legality.

G Guard Transition
Prevent a delta without an adequate disposition from changing the admitted governing state.

Mathematical analysis, PD, verification and reentry are selectable services used to discharge D/T/A when needed, not peer primitives of the guard.

## Hidden primitive

NewObject implies possible SemanticDelta.

SemanticDelta does not imply NewObject.

A new relation among old objects is a delta.
A new equation with identical semantics can have empty delta.
A renamed tool can have empty delta.
A hidden policy in prose can have nonempty delta without a new noun.

Therefore protection must be semantic-delta-sensitive rather than name-novelty-sensitive.

## Kernel candidate

The strongest candidate is the transition legality invariant:

A governing semantic change can enter admitted state only when it is typed, its disposition licenses that exact effect, and the relevant authority permits the effect.

The kernel need not contain MTA, PD, Multi-Object, Diagnosis, ARA or RTC.

Those are replaceable epistemic services.

The privileged obligation is that the transition cannot bypass admission.

## Tool routing

RTC after the object/job is typed enough for strict-gain search.

## Anti-regress

The guard does not require full MTA for every result.

No material governing delta means no expensive admission analysis.
A material governing delta receives proportional typing/adjudication.
Self-application repeats only after a material semantic delta.

Thus recursion is event-sensitive rather than unconditional.
