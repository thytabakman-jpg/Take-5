## Cross-project invariant

EXPAND is best at:
- discovering hidden alternatives/dependencies;
- separating conflated uncertainty coordinates;
- revealing responsibility and interaction structure.

FOCUS is best at:
- exact boundary placement;
- exact revision conditions;
- primitive-vs-composite discrimination;
- typed closure of a frozen question.

The combination:
EXPAND -> freeze observations -> RECONCILE -> FOCUS
provided a material payoff in all three projects.
