# Take-5 Research Records

**Job:** Navigate investigations, comparisons, experiments, and candidate findings used to evaluate the Take-5 tool/controller system.

## Entry points

- [Research reports in this folder](./) — observations, hypotheses, negative results and historical experiments.
- [Architecture and contracts](../architecture/) — separate specification and admission context.
- [Tool-system packages](../projects/tool-system/README.md) — current configured-tool navigation and owner mapping.
- [Integration records](../integration/) — current interfaces and earlier cross-system reconciliations.
- [Migration state](../MIGRATION_STATE.yaml) — repository-level migration state.

## Boundary

Research evidence is not, by location or report title, an active selector, executable capability, adopted theorem, or promotion decision. A claim used operationally must be checked against its exact version and authority source. Reaserch scientific projects remain separately typed; this area does not copy or supersede their results. No research report is moved by this README.
