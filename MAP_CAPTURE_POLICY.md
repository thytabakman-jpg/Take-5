# Mathematical Map Capture Policy

## Rule
Discovery status, epistemic status and current-use status are independent.

Captured(M) does not imply Accepted(M).

Every discovered mathematical candidate is captured before adjudication when practical. Rejected, superseded, overlapping, ignored and OPEN maps remain in lineage with provenance.

## Lifecycle
DISCOVER -> CAPTURE -> REGISTER -> CONNECT -> ATTACK -> RETAIN | REVISE | SPLIT | MERGE | SUPERSEDE | REJECT

Rejection changes epistemic/current-use status. It does not erase historical capture.

## Purpose
Prevent map loss, repeated rediscovery, missed connections and accidental filtering of uncertain mathematics.
